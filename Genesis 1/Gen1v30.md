# Exegetical Notes on Genesis 1:30

**Genesis 1:30 —** God gives every green plant as food to every beast of the earth, every bird of the heavens, and every creature that moves upon the earth.

---

## Hebrew Text

**וּלְכָל־חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ וּלְכָל־עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם וּלְכֹל רוֹמֵשׂ עַל־הָאָרֶץ אֲשֶׁר־בּוֹ נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה אֶת־כָּל־יֶרֶק עֵשֶׂב לְאָכְלָה וַיְהִי־כֵן׃**

---

## Transliteration

*ûləḵol-ḥayyaṯ hāʾāreṣ ûləḵol-ʿôp̄ haššāmayim ûləḵōl rōmēś ʿal-hāʾāreṣ ʾăšer-bô nep̄eš ḥayyāh ʾeṯ-kol-yereq ʿēśeḇ ləʾoḵlāh wayəhî-ḵēn*

---

## Literal Rendering

**“And to every beast of the earth, and to every bird of the heavens, and to everything moving upon the earth in which there is living life, every green plant for food; and it was so.”**

---

# 1. “And to Every Beast of the Earth”

The verse begins by extending God's provision beyond humanity.

Genesis 1:29 described food given to humanity.

Genesis 1:30 now identifies God's provision for the animal creation.

The Hebrew phrase:

**כָּל־חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ (*kol-ḥayyaṯ hāʾāreṣ*)**

means:

**“every beast of the earth.”**

---

# 2. God Provides for the Animals

The animals are not presented as existing outside God's providential care.

God provides food for them.

This reinforces the broader biblical theme that God sustains His creatures.

---

# 3. “Every Bird of the Heavens”

The next category is:

**וּלְכָל־עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם**

meaning:

**“and to every bird of the heavens.”**

The birds are included within God's provision.

---

# 4. “Everything Moving Upon the Earth”

The verse then broadens the description:

**וּלְכֹל רוֹמֵשׂ עַל־הָאָרֶץ**

meaning:

**“and to everything moving upon the earth.”**

This connects the provision with the creatures described throughout the creation account.

---

# 5. The Scope Is Comprehensive

The repeated use of **“every”** emphasizes the breadth of God's provision.

The categories include:

**beasts of the earth**

**birds of the heavens**

**creeping/moving creatures of the earth.**

---

# 6. “In Which There Is Living Life”

The phrase:

**אֲשֶׁר־בּוֹ נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה**

contains the expression:

**נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה (*nep̄eš ḥayyāh*)**

commonly rendered:

**“living creature”**

or literally:

**“living soul/life.”**

---

# 7. *Nephesh Chayyah*

The expression **נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה** has already appeared in Genesis 1.

It is associated with living creatures brought forth by God.

The expression therefore emphasizes living creatures rather than merely physical objects.

---

# 8. Animals Are Living Creatures

Genesis identifies the animals as living creatures created by God.

Their life comes within God's created order.

---

# 9. God Gives Food to Living Creatures

The verse connects:

**life**

with

**God's provision.**

The creatures live within an environment supplied by their Creator.

---

# 10. “Every Green Plant”

The food assigned to these creatures is:

**כָּל־יֶרֶק עֵשֶׂב**

meaning approximately:

**“every green plant.”**

The word **יֶרֶק (*yereq*)** refers to green vegetation.

The word **עֵשֶׂב (*ʿēśeḇ*)** refers to vegetation, grass, or plants.

---

# 11. Plant Provision for Animals

God gives vegetation as food for the animal creation.

Thus Genesis 1:29 and 1:30 together present a broad plant-based provision.

---

# 12. Humanity and Animals

The preceding verse gave seed-bearing plants and fruit-bearing trees to humanity.

This verse gives green vegetation to the animals.

---

# 13. A Provision Structure

The two verses can therefore be viewed together:

**Genesis 1:29**

Food for humanity.

**Genesis 1:30**

Food for animals.

---

# 14. God Provides for All His Creatures

The Creator provides according to the needs of different creatures.

Humanity receives food.

Animals receive vegetation.

---

# 15. Divine Providence

Genesis 1:30 gives another picture of divine providence.

God not only creates animals.

He creates an environment capable of sustaining them.

---

# 16. Creation Is Ordered Toward Life

The verse reinforces the orderly character of creation.

Living creatures are provided with appropriate nourishment.

---

# 17. Provision Is Part of the Created Order

Food is not an afterthought.

The provision of nourishment belongs to God's original arrangement of creation.

---

# 18. The Earth Sustains Life by God's Design

Vegetation provides nourishment for animal life.

This relationship is part of the created order established by God.

---

# 19. Animals Depend Upon Creation

Animals depend upon vegetation for food.

This establishes a relationship within the created ecosystem.

---

# 20. Animals Ultimately Depend Upon God

Although animals eat vegetation, Genesis presents God as the ultimate provider.

The food exists because God created and ordered the world.

---

# 21. The Creator and the Creature

The relationship can be expressed:

**God → provides**

**vegetation → nourishes**

**animals → receive and live.**

---

# 22. God Is the Source of Life

The repeated emphasis on living creatures ultimately directs attention to God as Creator.

---

# 23. “And It Was So” — וַיְהִי־כֵן (*wayəhî-kēn*)

The verse ends with:

**וַיְהִי־כֵן**

meaning:

**“and it was so.”**

This expression has appeared repeatedly throughout Genesis 1.

---

# 24. Divine Word and Fulfillment

God speaks.

The created order corresponds to God's command.

The repeated:

**“and it was so”**

emphasizes the effectiveness of God's word.

---

# 25. God's Word Accomplishes His Purpose

Genesis presents God's speech as effective.

What God commands comes to pass.

---

# 26. The Sovereignty of God

The verse therefore contributes to the doctrine of divine sovereignty.

God determines the order of creation.

---

# 27. God's Authority Over Creation

Animals and vegetation exist within an order established by God.

---

# 28. Provision Is Not Random

The food relationships described in the verse are part of God's intentional ordering of creation.

---

# 29. The Goodness of God's Provision

Genesis 1 has repeatedly described creation as good.

The provision of food contributes to the flourishing of created life.

---

# 30. Life Is Sustained

God's creation includes means by which living creatures can continue to live.

---

# 31. Creation Is Generative

Plants reproduce and provide food.

Animals consume vegetation.

The created order is capable of continuing beyond the initial act of creation.

---

# 32. The Seed Principle Continues

The previous verse emphasized seed-bearing plants.

This verse emphasizes green vegetation as animal food.

Together they demonstrate a productive and sustaining creation.

---

# 33. The Food Chain in Genesis 1

At this stage of the narrative, the text describes a relationship in which vegetation provides food for living creatures.

It does not describe animals receiving one another as food.

---

# 34. Genesis 1:30 and Diet

The verse explicitly identifies:

**green vegetation**

as food for the animals described.

This is the food provision stated in the passage.

---

# 35. The Initial Created Order

Within Genesis 1, God's stated food provision for animals is vegetation.

This observation should be kept distinct from later biblical passages that describe additional developments.

---

# 36. Genesis 9:3

Later, after the Flood, God tells Noah that animals can also serve as food for humanity.

Genesis 1:30 should therefore be interpreted according to its own place in the biblical narrative.

---

# 37. Do Not Import Later Conditions Into This Verse

The primary exegetical point of Genesis 1:30 is God's provision of green vegetation for the living creatures.

Later developments should not obscure that immediate meaning.

---

# 38. God's Care Extends Beyond Humanity

The verse is significant because God's provision is not restricted to humans.

Animals also receive provision.

---

# 39. Creation Has Value Before Humanity

Animals exist as God's creatures independently of their usefulness to humans.

---

# 40. Animals Belong to God's Created Order

They are created by God and sustained within the environment He establishes.

---

# 41. Human Dominion Does Not Eliminate God's Care

Genesis 1:28 gives humanity dominion over animals.

Genesis 1:30 shows that these same animals remain under God's providential care.

---

# 42. Dominion and Stewardship

Human dominion therefore exists alongside God's continuing care for the creatures.

---

# 43. Human Responsibility Toward Animals

If God provides for animals, human dominion should not be understood as permission for needless cruelty or destruction.

---

# 44. God's Interest in His Creation

The provision of food demonstrates God's concern for the living creatures He has made.

---

# 45. Animal Life Matters

Animals are not presented as meaningless objects.

They are living creatures within God's creation.

---

# 46. Theological Significance of “Living”

The expression **נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה** emphasizes the living character of these creatures.

---

# 47. Creaturely Life

The animals possess creaturely life given by God.

This distinguishes living creatures from inanimate objects.

---

# 48. God Sustains Creaturely Life

The provision of food is one means by which that life is sustained.

---

# 49. Providence Through Ordinary Means

Genesis portrays God's care through ordinary created means.

Vegetation becomes food.

---

# 50. God's Sovereignty and Secondary Means

God remains sovereign while using the created order as the means through which creatures receive nourishment.

---

# 51. Creation Is an Ordered System

The verse contributes to the picture of creation as an interconnected system.

---

# 52. Different Creatures, Different Provisions

Genesis 1 distinguishes between different categories of creatures while describing God's provision for them.

---

# 53. Humanity and Animals Share Dependence

Both humanity and animals depend upon God's provision.

---

# 54. Humanity Is Still Distinct

Although humans and animals are both living creatures, Genesis 1:27 uniquely identifies humanity as God's image-bearer.

---

# 55. Shared Creatureliness

Humanity and animals share creaturely dependence.

---

# 56. Unique Human Dignity

Humanity nevertheless possesses a unique status because humanity is created in God's image.

---

# 57. The Balance of Genesis 1

Genesis simultaneously teaches:

**human uniqueness**

and

**human creatureliness.**

---

# 58. Humanity Is Not Autonomous

Humans depend upon God's provision just as animals do.

---

# 59. Animals Are Not Human

The fact that animals are living creatures does not make them image-bearers in the same sense as humanity.

---

# 60. Distinct Orders Within Creation

Genesis maintains distinctions among:

**plants**

**animals**

**humanity.**

---

# 61. The Created Hierarchy

The broader structure of Genesis 1 places humanity in a unique position of dominion while remaining under God.

---

# 62. Dominion Is Not Absolute

Human authority over animals exists within God's larger sovereignty.

---

# 63. The Earth as God's Provision

Vegetation serves as a means through which God sustains animal life.

---

# 64. Creation as Gift

The natural world is therefore not merely raw material.

It is part of God's provision.

---

# 65. Stewardship and Ecology

The verse provides a theological foundation for thinking about ecological responsibility.

God cares for living creatures.

Humans are entrusted with dominion.

Therefore human use of creation should be responsible.

---

# 66. Creation Care Is Not Creation Worship

Care for creation does not require treating creation as divine.

Genesis maintains the distinction between:

**Creator**

and

**creation.**

---

# 67. Worship Belongs to God

The proper response to God's provision is worship and gratitude toward the Creator.

---

# 68. Gratitude for the Created Order

The ordered relationship between vegetation and animals reveals God's wisdom and generosity.

---

# 69. God's Wisdom

The creation account repeatedly presents God's ordering of creation as purposeful.

---

# 70. Provision and Wisdom

The provision of food demonstrates practical wisdom within the created order.

---

# 71. The Creator Knows His Creatures

God provides according to the life He has created.

---

# 72. Life and Environment

The verse implicitly connects living creatures with their environment.

Animals require an environment containing suitable food.

God provides that environment.

---

# 73. The Earth Is Habitable

Genesis presents the earth as prepared for life.

---

# 74. God's Preparation

Vegetation exists before the animals are fully established in the creation sequence.

This creates an ordered environment in which animal life can be sustained.

---

# 75. Provision Before Need

God provides vegetation as part of creation before the animals' continued life depends upon it.

---

# 76. Divine Foresight

The sequence illustrates God's purposeful ordering of creation.

---

# 77. God's Care Is Comprehensive

The repeated categories show that divine provision extends broadly across animal life.

---

# 78. “Every”

The repetition of comprehensive terms is important:

**every beast**

**every bird**

**everything moving**

**every green plant.**

---

# 79. Comprehensive Provision

The verse emphasizes that God's provision is not narrowly restricted.

---

# 80. The Scope of Divine Providence

God's providence encompasses the entire created order described in the passage.

---

# 81. Creation Is Dependent Upon God

Every creature ultimately depends upon the Creator.

---

# 82. Human Dominion and Divine Providence

Humanity rules over animals under God's authority.

Animals remain creatures sustained by God.

---

# 83. Theological Balance

Genesis 1:30 therefore prevents two opposite errors:

**denying human authority**

and

**treating human authority as absolute.**

---

# 84. Human Authority Is Delegated

Humanity receives dominion from God.

---

# 85. God's Authority Is Absolute

God remains the Creator and provider of all living things.

---

# 86. The Verse and Divine Goodness

God's provision is consistent with the repeated declaration that creation is good.

---

# 87. Life Is Sustained Within Good Creation

The animal world is provided with food within the good order established by God.

---

# 88. The Verse and God's Generosity

God's generosity is not limited to humanity.

He provides for animals as well.

---

# 89. The Creator's Care

Genesis therefore presents a Creator who creates, orders, blesses, and provides.

---

# 90. Creation and Providence

Genesis 1:30 forms a bridge between:

**creation**

and

**providence.**

God creates the creatures and provides for their continued existence.

---

# 91. The Importance of “And It Was So”

The final phrase confirms that God's word successfully establishes the intended order.

---

# 92. Divine Effectiveness

God speaks and creation responds.

---

# 93. Divine Sovereignty

The created order is ultimately subject to God's command.

---

# 94. God's Word and Creation

The repeated pattern throughout Genesis 1 is:

**God said**

→

**God's purpose was accomplished**

→

**creation existed according to His word.**

---

# 95. The Verse as a Conclusion to the Food Provision

Genesis 1:29 gives humanity's food.

Genesis 1:30 gives the animals' food.

The two verses complete the immediate description of food provision before the concluding evaluation of creation.

---

# 96. Preparation for Genesis 1:31

Genesis 1:30 ends with:

**“And it was so.”**

Genesis 1:31 will then provide God's final assessment of the completed creation.

---

# 97. The Progression of Genesis 1

The narrative has moved from:

**formless creation**

to

**ordered creation**

to

**living creatures**

to

**humanity**

to

**provision for life.**

---

# 98. Theological Summary

Genesis 1:30 teaches that God provided green vegetation as food for the animals and other living creatures of the earth, demonstrating His providential care and the ordered goodness of creation.

---

# 99. The Central Theological Proposition

If Genesis 1:30 is reduced to its fundamental theological assertion, it could be stated this way:

**God provided green vegetation as food for the living creatures of the earth, demonstrating His comprehensive care and sustaining provision within His ordered creation.**

Or more simply:

**God provides for every living creature, sustaining animal life through the good and ordered provision of creation.**

---

# Exegetical Outline

## Genesis 1:30

### **“Every beast of the earth”**

→ God's provision extends to terrestrial animals.

### **“Every bird of the heavens”**

→ God's provision extends to birds.

### **“Everything moving upon the earth”**

→ God's provision extends broadly to other living creatures.

### **“In which there is living life”**

→ These are living creatures sustained by God.

### **“Every green plant”**

→ Vegetation is appointed as their food.

### **“For food”**

→ God's creation contains provision for creaturely life.

### **“And it was so”**

→ God's spoken purpose was accomplished.

---

# Doctrinal Implications

Genesis 1:30 establishes or contributes to these major doctrines:

1. **God provides for His creatures** — divine care extends beyond humanity.
2. **God sustains animal life** — living creatures receive nourishment within God's created order.
3. **Creation is ordered** — vegetation provides food for animal life.
4. **Creation is good** — God's provision occurs within the good creation established in Genesis 1.
5. **Animals are living creatures** — the text explicitly identifies them with living life.
6. **Animals have creaturely value** — they are creatures made and sustained by God.
7. **Humanity is not the only object of divine providence** — God provides for animals as well.
8. **Human dominion is subordinate to God** — humans rule animals while God remains their Creator and Provider.
9. **Human dominion requires responsibility** — God's care for animals provides a basis for responsible stewardship.
10. **Vegetation sustains animal life** — green plants are identified as animal food.
11. **The initial food provision is plant-based** — Genesis 1:30 identifies vegetation as food for the creatures described.
12. **The created order is productive** — plants provide continuing nourishment.
13. **God uses created means to sustain life** — vegetation functions as an instrument of divine provision.
14. **God's word is effective** — “and it was so” confirms the accomplishment of His command.
15. **God is sovereign over creation** — the created order exists according to His command.
16. **Humanity and animals share creaturely dependence** — both depend ultimately upon God's provision.
17. **Humanity remains uniquely God's image-bearer** — shared creatureliness does not erase humanity's unique status in Genesis 1:27.
18. **Creation care has theological grounding** — God's provision for animals gives weight to responsible human stewardship.
19. **Creation is not autonomous** — the created order depends upon its Creator.
20. **Divine providence begins within creation itself** — God's sustaining care is already evident in Genesis 1.
21. **God's generosity is comprehensive** — His provision extends across the animal creation.
22. **Life and environment are connected** — God creates a world capable of sustaining living creatures.
23. **Provision precedes continued flourishing** — God establishes food within the created environment.
24. **God's care is purposeful** — provision serves the continuation of creaturely life.
25. **Creation points toward the Creator** — the ordered provision of life reveals God's wisdom, authority, and generosity.

# A Concise Preaching/Exposition Framework

## I. GOD PROVIDES FOR EVERY LIVING CREATURE

**“Every beast… every bird… everything moving…”**

God's providence extends throughout the living creation.

---

## II. GOD PROVIDES THROUGH THE CREATED ORDER

**“Every green plant…”**

God uses vegetation as a means of sustaining animal life.

---

## III. GOD'S CREATION IS ORDERED TOWARD LIFE

The creatures receive appropriate provision within the environment God established.

---

## IV. GOD'S WORD ACCOMPLISHES HIS PURPOSE

**“And it was so.”**

What God commands comes to pass.

---

# Main Proposition

**Genesis 1:30 teaches that God provided green vegetation as food for the living creatures of the earth, demonstrating His comprehensive providential care and the ordered goodness of His creation.**

The progression from Genesis 1:28 to Genesis 1:30 is:

**Genesis 1:28**

God blesses humanity and gives humanity the mandate to:

**be fruitful**

**multiply**

**fill the earth**

**subdue it**

**rule over living creatures.**

**Genesis 1:29**

God provides food for humanity:

**seed-bearing plants**

**fruit-bearing trees.**

**Genesis 1:30**

God provides food for the animals:

**green vegetation.**

The pattern is:

**God creates.**

**God blesses.**

**God commissions.**

**God provides for humanity.**

**God provides for the animals.**

**God sustains the life of His creation.**

**And it was so.**
