# Exegetical Notes on Genesis 1:28

**Genesis 1:28 —** God blesses humanity, commands them to be fruitful and multiply, fill the earth and subdue it, and exercise dominion over the living creatures.

---

## Hebrew Text

**וַיְבָרֶךְ אֹתָם אֱלֹהִים וַיֹּאמֶר לָהֶם אֱלֹהִים פְּרוּ וּרְבוּ וּמִלְאוּ אֶת־הָאָרֶץ וְכִבְשֻׁהָ וּרְדוּ בִּדְגַת הַיָּם וּבְעוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם וּבְכָל־חַיָּה הָרֹמֶשֶׂת עַל־הָאָרֶץ׃**

---

## Transliteration

*Wayḇāreḵ ʾōṯām ʾĕlōhîm wayyōʾmer lāhem ʾĕlōhîm pərû ûrḇû ûmilʾû ʾeṯ-hāʾāreṣ wəḵiḇšuhā ûrədû biḏgaṯ hayyām ûḇəʿôp̄ haššāmayim ûḇəḵol-ḥayyāh hārōmeśeṯ ʿal-hāʾāreṣ*

---

## Literal Rendering

**“And God blessed them, and God said to them, ‘Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it; and rule over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living creature that moves upon the earth.’”**

---

# 1. “And God Blessed Them” — וַיְבָרֶךְ אֹתָם אֱלֹהִים (*wayḇāreḵ ʾōṯām ʾĕlōhîm*)

Genesis 1:28 begins with God's blessing.

The Hebrew verb is:

**בָּרַךְ (*bāraḵ*)**

meaning:

**“to bless.”**

God is the subject.

Humanity is the recipient.

---

# 2. Blessing Precedes the Command

The order is significant:

**God blessed them → God spoke to them → God commissioned them.**

Humanity's vocation begins with divine blessing.

---

# 3. God's Blessing Is Not Merely a Wish

Biblical blessing is more than expressing goodwill.

When God blesses His creatures, the blessing communicates divine favor and enables the recipient to fulfill the purpose God gives.

---

# 4. The Blessing Is Given to “Them”

The plural pronoun:

**אֹתָם (*ʾōṯām*)**

means:

**“them.”**

The blessing therefore encompasses both male and female introduced in verse 27.

---

# 5. Male and Female Receive the Blessing

Genesis 1:27 ends with:

**“male and female he created them.”**

Genesis 1:28 immediately says:

**“God blessed them.”**

The connection is direct.

Both male and female receive God's blessing.

---

# 6. “And God Said to Them”

God does not merely bless humanity.

He speaks to humanity.

The divine blessing is accompanied by divine instruction.

---

# 7. Humanity Receives a Divine Commission

Genesis 1:28 therefore contains humanity's first recorded commission.

Humanity is given a task within God's creation.

---

# 8. “Be Fruitful” — פְּרוּ (*pərû*)

The Hebrew verb:

**פָּרָה (*pārâ*)**

means:

**“to be fruitful”**

**“to bear fruit”**

or

**“to produce abundantly.”**

The imperative:

**פְּרוּ**

means:

**“Be fruitful.”**

---

# 9. Fruitfulness

The command concerns the multiplication and flourishing of human life.

Humanity is commanded to produce offspring.

---

# 10. “Multiply” — וּרְבוּ (*ûrḇû*)

The verb:

**רָבָה (*rāḇâ*)**

means:

**“to become many”**

**“to increase”**

or

**“to multiply.”**

The imperative:

**וּרְבוּ**

means:

**“and multiply.”**

---

# 11. Fruitfulness and Multiplication

The two commands work together:

**be fruitful**

and

**multiply.**

Humanity is commissioned to populate the earth.

---

# 12. Human Reproduction Is Part of Creation

The command shows that reproduction is not presented as something outside God's original design.

Human multiplication belongs to the created order.

---

# 13. The Blessing and the Command

Notice the relationship:

**God blesses humanity**

→

**God commands humanity**

→

**humanity is commissioned to reproduce and fill the earth.**

---

# 14. “Fill the Earth” — וּמִלְאוּ אֶת־הָאָרֶץ (*ûmilʾû ʾeṯ-hāʾāreṣ*)

The verb:

**מָלֵא (*mālēʾ*)**

means:

**“to fill”**

**“to be full”**

or

**“to fill up.”**

The command means that humanity is to spread throughout the earth.

---

# 15. The Earth Is Humanity's Sphere

The command expands humanity's presence beyond a single location.

Humanity is intended to inhabit the earth.

---

# 16. Humanity Is Meant to Spread

The creation mandate is therefore geographic as well as demographic.

Humanity is to:

**multiply**

and

**fill the earth.**

---

# 17. “Subdue It” — וְכִבְשֻׁהָ (*wəḵiḇšuhā*)

The Hebrew verb:

**כָּבַשׁ (*kāḇaš*)**

means:

**“to subdue”**

**“to bring under control”**

or

**“to bring into submission.”**

The command is strong language.

---

# 18. Subduing the Earth

The command indicates that humanity is to bring the earth under ordered human cultivation and use.

This develops the dominion assignment introduced in verse 26.

---

# 19. Subduing Does Not Mean Destroying

The command should not be interpreted as authorization for arbitrary destruction.

Genesis has already established that God's creation is good.

Humanity is therefore to exercise purposeful authority over a good creation.

---

# 20. Dominion Is Delegated

Humanity does not receive absolute ownership.

God remains the Creator.

Humanity receives authority from God.

---

# 21. “Rule” — וּרְדוּ (*ûrədû*)

The verb:

**רָדָה (*rādâ*)**

means:

**“to rule”**

**“to have dominion”**

or

**“to exercise authority.”**

It is the same fundamental verb associated with the dominion assignment in Genesis 1:26.

---

# 22. Verse 28 Expands Verse 26

Genesis 1:26 says:

**“Let them have dominion.”**

Genesis 1:28 now gives the command:

**“Rule.”**

Verse 28 therefore explains how humanity's image-bearing vocation is to be carried out.

---

# 23. Dominion Is a Command

Human rule over creation is not merely a description of what humans happen to do.

It is part of God's commission.

---

# 24. The Scope of Dominion

The command specifically identifies:

**fish of the sea**

**birds of the heavens**

**every living creature moving upon the earth.**

---

# 25. Fish of the Sea

Humanity's authority extends to aquatic creatures.

---

# 26. Birds of the Heavens

Humanity's authority extends to creatures of the sky.

---

# 27. Every Living Creature

The scope then encompasses terrestrial living creatures.

The mandate is comprehensive.

---

# 28. The Creation Mandate

Genesis 1:28 is commonly described as the:

**Creation Mandate**

or

**Cultural Mandate.**

The verse establishes the fundamental human vocation within God's created world.

---

# 29. The Core Elements of the Mandate

The command can be summarized in five movements:

**Be fruitful**

**Multiply**

**Fill the earth**

**Subdue it**

**Rule over living creatures**

---

# 30. Fruitfulness

Humanity is commanded to increase numerically.

---

# 31. Multiplication

Human population growth is incorporated into God's original commission.

---

# 32. Filling the Earth

Humanity is to inhabit the earth comprehensively.

---

# 33. Subduing the Earth

Humanity is to exercise ordered authority over the earth.

---

# 34. Ruling the Creatures

Humanity is to exercise dominion over the animal creation.

---

# 35. Humanity's Royal Vocation

Genesis 1:28 develops the royal dimension of humanity's identity.

Humanity is created in God's image and commissioned to rule under God.

---

# 36. Image and Dominion

The immediate sequence of verses is crucial:

**Genesis 1:26** — image and dominion.

**Genesis 1:27** — male and female created in God's image.

**Genesis 1:28** — blessing and dominion commission.

The three verses belong together.

---

# 37. Image-Bearing and Vocation

Humanity's identity and mission are inseparable.

Human beings are created as God's image-bearers and given a corresponding vocation.

---

# 38. God's Representative Rulers

Humanity functions as God's representative within creation.

The authority is real, but it is derived.

---

# 39. Delegated Kingship

Human dominion can therefore be described as:

**delegated kingship.**

Humanity rules because God appoints humanity to rule.

---

# 40. God Remains King

The Creator never surrenders His ultimate sovereignty.

Human authority remains subordinate to God's authority.

---

# 41. Blessing and Responsibility

The blessing of Genesis 1:28 is connected to responsibility.

God's blessing does not remove human obligation.

It empowers humanity for its calling.

---

# 42. The Earth Is a Gift and a Task

The earth is given as humanity's sphere of activity.

But it is also entrusted to humanity for responsible cultivation.

---

# 43. Stewardship

The idea of stewardship follows naturally from delegated dominion.

Humanity manages what ultimately belongs to God.

---

# 44. Creation Care

Because humanity rules God's creation, human beings have a responsibility to care for it.

The mandate cannot properly be reduced to exploitation.

---

# 45. Human Culture

The Creation Mandate also provides the foundation for human cultural development.

As humanity fills the earth and exercises dominion, humans develop:

**families**

**communities**

**agriculture**

**technology**

**art**

**institutions**

**knowledge**

and other forms of culture.

---

# 46. Work Is Part of Humanity's Original Calling

Human work is therefore not introduced merely as a consequence of sin.

Humanity receives a work-like commission before the Fall.

---

# 47. The Fall and Work

Genesis 3 later introduces hardship and frustration into human labor.

But the basic vocation to cultivate and exercise responsible dominion precedes the Fall.

---

# 48. Culture Before the Fall

Human civilization therefore has its foundation in God's original commission.

The development of culture can be understood as part of humanity's response to God's mandate.

---

# 49. Technology and Dominion

Technology can be understood as one means through which humanity exercises dominion.

But technology remains subject to moral responsibility.

---

# 50. Agriculture and Dominion

Agriculture provides an obvious example of the mandate:

**cultivating the earth**

**producing food**

**developing land**

**sustaining human communities.**

---

# 51. Dominion and Wisdom

Humanity's authority requires wisdom.

Power without wisdom can become destructive.

---

# 52. Dominion and Goodness

God's creation is declared good.

Humanity's rule should therefore seek the good order and flourishing of creation.

---

# 53. Dominion Is Not Tyranny

Biblical dominion should not be equated with arbitrary oppression.

Human authority is modeled under God's authority.

---

# 54. The Pattern of Divine Rule

God's rule in Genesis 1 is characterized by:

**order**

**purpose**

**separation**

**provision**

**goodness**

**life.**

Human dominion should reflect these characteristics.

---

# 55. Human Dominion Has Boundaries

Human beings receive authority over the earth and animals.

They do not receive authority to become God.

---

# 56. The Creator-Creature Distinction

The hierarchy remains:

**God → Creator**

**humanity → image-bearing ruler**

**animals and earth → created realm entrusted to humanity.**

---

# 57. Human Dominion Is Accountable

Because God gives the command, humanity remains accountable to God for how dominion is exercised.

---

# 58. The Blessing Is Corporate

The command is addressed to:

**“them.”**

The vocation belongs to humanity collectively.

---

# 59. Male and Female Together

The text does not give the dominion mandate exclusively to the male.

Genesis 1:27 identifies both male and female.

Verse 28 then says:

**“God blessed them.”**

---

# 60. Shared Human Vocation

Both male and female participate in humanity's creational vocation.

---

# 61. Fruitfulness and Family

The command to be fruitful and multiply necessarily establishes human family expansion as part of the original creation order.

---

# 62. Population and God's Purpose

Human population is not portrayed as inherently contrary to God's purposes.

The original command assumes humanity will increase and fill the earth.

---

# 63. Human Expansion

The command to fill the earth establishes a global horizon for humanity.

Humanity is not designed to remain confined to one small region.

---

# 64. Global Human Community

The command anticipates a world filled with human communities.

---

# 65. The Earth Is Humanity's Sphere of Vocation

Everywhere humanity lives, the basic creation mandate remains relevant:

**cultivate**

**develop**

**build**

**govern**

**care**

**create.**

---

# 66. The Mandate and Human Creativity

Human creativity reflects the unique capacities associated with being God's image-bearers.

---

# 67. Human Creativity and God's Creativity

God creates.

Humanity, as God's image-bearer, creates culture, tools, structures, art, and knowledge from the resources of God's creation.

Human creativity is therefore derivative rather than absolute.

---

# 68. Humanity Does Not Create *Ex Nihilo*

God creates in the ultimate sense.

Human beings transform and cultivate the created world.

---

# 69. Cultivation Rather Than Replacement

Human cultural activity should work with God's creation rather than attempt to replace God's authority.

---

# 70. Dominion and Civilization

The development of civilization can therefore be viewed through the framework of Genesis 1:28.

---

# 71. Civilization Has Moral Direction

Not everything humans create is automatically good.

The Creation Mandate establishes responsibility, not moral approval of every human achievement.

---

# 72. The Fall Distorts Dominion

Genesis 3 introduces sin into humanity's relationship with God, one another, and creation.

Human dominion can therefore become abusive.

---

# 73. Sinful Dominion

After the Fall, human authority may become:

**exploitative**

**violent**

**self-centered**

**oppressive**

**destructive.**

Genesis 1:28 itself, however, establishes the original vocation before sin.

---

# 74. Redemption and Dominion

The biblical story later presents God's redemptive work as restoring humanity to faithful service under God.

---

# 75. Christ and Human Vocation

The New Testament presents Christ as the true image of God and the rightful ruler.

This provides an important framework for understanding humanity's original vocation.

---

# 76. Humanity's Failure

The biblical story demonstrates that humanity does not faithfully fulfill its original calling because of sin.

---

# 77. The Need for Restoration

The failure of humanity creates the need for redemption and restoration.

---

# 78. Creation and Redemption

Biblical theology therefore connects:

**creation**

**Fall**

**redemption**

**restoration.**

Genesis 1:28 belongs to the foundational creation stage of this story.

---

# 79. The Creation Mandate and the Great Commission

The Creation Mandate should not simply be equated with the Great Commission.

They are distinct commands in different covenantal contexts.

However, both involve humanity fulfilling God's purposes throughout the earth.

---

# 80. Creation Mandate

Genesis 1:28 concerns:

**human multiplication**

**earthly habitation**

**cultivation**

**dominion.**

---

# 81. Great Commission

The Great Commission concerns:

**making disciples**

**baptizing**

**teaching Christ's commands**

and the worldwide proclamation of the gospel.

---

# 82. Distinct but Related

Christian theology can therefore recognize both:

**the original creation vocation**

and

**the redemptive mission of the church.**

---

# 83. God's Blessing

The command begins with blessing.

This is important for understanding humanity's vocation.

Human beings do not begin their mission under condemnation.

They begin under divine blessing.

---

# 84. Creation Is Initially Good

Genesis 1:28 occurs before sin enters the narrative.

The original human vocation is therefore part of God's good creation.

---

# 85. Humanity's First Commission

Genesis 1:28 can be understood as humanity's first commission from God.

It establishes:

**identity**

**purpose**

**responsibility**

**direction.**

---

# 86. Human Purpose

Humanity's purpose is not merely survival.

Humanity is created to:

**multiply**

**fill**

**cultivate**

**subdue**

**rule.**

---

# 87. Purposeful Existence

Genesis therefore presents human existence as teleological.

Human beings have a God-given purpose.

---

# 88. Humanity Is Blessed to Work

The blessing is connected to productive activity.

Human labor belongs to humanity's original vocation.

---

# 89. Productivity

Fruitfulness and dominion imply productive engagement with God's creation.

---

# 90. Responsible Development

The earth is not merely to be occupied.

It is to be cultivated and developed under God's authority.

---

# 91. The Earth as God's Garden

Genesis 2 will provide a more localized picture of humanity's agricultural vocation.

Genesis 1:28 provides the broader global mandate.

---

# 92. Humanity and the Land

Human beings are connected to the earth while simultaneously being commissioned to exercise authority over it.

---

# 93. The Balance

Genesis 1:28 establishes two realities:

**humanity belongs to creation**

and

**humanity rules within creation.**

---

# 94. Creaturely Dominion

Humanity rules as a creature.

This distinction prevents both human self-deification and contempt for creation.

---

# 95. The Earth Is Not Humanity's Absolute Possession

Humanity is entrusted with the earth.

God remains the ultimate Creator and Lord.

---

# 96. Dominion and Justice

Because human authority comes from God, it should be exercised justly.

---

# 97. Dominion and Human Community

Human dominion also involves governing human communities.

The broader biblical development of human society will involve:

**family**

**government**

**work**

**law**

**justice**

**community.**

---

# 98. The Cultural Mandate

The term **“Cultural Mandate”** summarizes the broad responsibility humanity receives to cultivate and develop the created world under God.

---

# 99. The Central Theological Proposition

If Genesis 1:28 is reduced to its fundamental theological assertion, it could be stated this way:

**God blessed humanity and commissioned male and female to multiply, fill the earth, cultivate and subdue it, and exercise responsible dominion over its living creatures.**

Or more simply:

**God blessed humanity to fill the earth and faithfully exercise delegated dominion over His creation.**

---

# Exegetical Outline

## Genesis 1:28

### **“God blessed them”**

→ Humanity's vocation begins with divine blessing.

### **“God said to them”**

→ Humanity receives a direct divine commission.

### **“Be fruitful”**

→ Humanity is commanded to produce offspring.

### **“Multiply”**

→ Humanity is commanded to increase in number.

### **“Fill the earth”**

→ Humanity is commanded to inhabit the earth.

### **“Subdue it”**

→ Humanity is commanded to bring the earth under ordered and productive human stewardship.

### **“Rule over…”**

→ Humanity receives delegated authority over the animal creation.

### **“Fish… birds… every living creature”**

→ The scope of dominion extends across the major realms of animal life.

---

# Doctrinal Implications

Genesis 1:28 establishes or contributes to these major doctrines:

1. **Humanity is blessed by God** — the original human vocation begins under divine blessing.
2. **Humanity receives a divine commission** — human life has a God-given purpose.
3. **Fruitfulness is part of the creation mandate** — humanity is commanded to reproduce.
4. **Multiplication is part of God's original purpose** — humanity is commanded to increase.
5. **Humanity is commissioned to fill the earth** — the mandate has a global geographical scope.
6. **Humanity is commissioned to subdue the earth** — humans are given authority to cultivate and develop creation.
7. **Humanity receives dominion over animals** — the authority introduced in Genesis 1:26 is formally commanded.
8. **Dominion is delegated** — human authority originates from God.
9. **God remains the ultimate sovereign** — human dominion does not replace God's authority.
10. **Humanity has a stewardship responsibility** — authority must be exercised responsibly.
11. **Human work precedes the Fall** — productive activity is part of God's original design.
12. **Human culture has creational roots** — civilization and cultivation belong to humanity's original vocation.
13. **Human creativity has a creational foundation** — image-bearing humanity is commissioned to develop the created world.
14. **Male and female share the mandate** — the blessing is given to the humanity described in verse 27.
15. **Human sexuality has a creational context** — fruitfulness and multiplication are embedded within the original human vocation.
16. **The earth is good** — humanity's task concerns God's good creation.
17. **Dominion is not absolute ownership** — humanity remains creaturely and accountable to God.
18. **Human authority carries moral responsibility** — power must be exercised according to God's purposes.
19. **The Creation Mandate precedes the Fall** — humanity's vocation is established before sin enters the world.
20. **Sin later distorts human dominion** — Genesis 1:28 provides the original standard against which distorted dominion can be understood.
21. **Humanity has a global vocation** — the mandate encompasses the entire earth.
22. **Human existence is purposeful** — humanity is created for a defined mission.
23. **Blessing and responsibility are connected** — God's blessing accompanies humanity's commission.
24. **Humanity represents God's rule** — dominion flows from humanity's image-bearing identity.
25. **Creation and redemption belong to one biblical storyline** — the original mandate provides an important foundation for understanding humanity's later need for restoration.

---

# A Concise Preaching/Exposition Framework

## I. GOD BLESSES HUMANITY

**“God blessed them…”**

Humanity's vocation begins not with abandonment but with divine blessing.

---

## II. GOD COMMISSIONS HUMANITY TO FILL THE EARTH

**“Be fruitful and multiply…”**

Humanity is called to reproduce, increase, and inhabit the earth.

---

## III. GOD COMMISSIONS HUMANITY TO CULTIVATE THE EARTH

**“Fill the earth and subdue it…”**

Humanity is entrusted with the productive development and ordered cultivation of God's creation.

---

## IV. GOD COMMISSIONS HUMANITY TO RULE CREATION

**“Rule over…”**

Human beings receive delegated authority over the living creatures.

---

# Main Proposition

**Genesis 1:28 teaches that God blessed humanity and commissioned male and female to multiply, fill the earth, cultivate it, and exercise responsible dominion over the living creatures as God's image-bearing representatives.**

The progression from Genesis 1:26 to Genesis 1:28 is:

**Genesis 1:26**

God announces:

**“Let us make man…”**

Humanity is created:

**“in our image.”**

Humanity receives:

**dominion.**

**Genesis 1:27**

God creates humanity:

**“in his own image.”**

Humanity is created:

**male and female.**

**Genesis 1:28**

God:

**blesses them**

and commands them to:

**be fruitful**

**multiply**

**fill the earth**

**subdue it**

**rule over living creatures.**

The pattern is:

**God creates humanity.**

**God gives humanity His image.**

**God blesses humanity.**

**God gives humanity a mission.**

**God entrusts humanity with dominion.**

**Humanity is to exercise that dominion under God's authority.**
